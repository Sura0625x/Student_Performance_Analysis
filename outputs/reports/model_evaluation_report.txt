4. ACADEMIC INTERPRETATION OF METRICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Accuracy: The overall percentage of correct predictions (both Pass and Fail)
  out of all tested students. Formula: (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN).

• Precision: Out of all students predicted as 'Pass', what fraction actually passed?
  High precision means very few false alarms. Formula: TP / (TP + FP).

• Recall (Sensitivity): Out of all students who actually passed, what fraction did
  the model successfully identify? Formula: TP / (TP + FN).

• F1-Score: The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. It provides a balanced
  evaluation especially when classes are unevenly distributed.
  Formula: 2 * (Precision * Recall) / (Precision + Recall).

• Information Gain (Entropy): Measures the reduction in uncertainty/impurity
  achieved when splitting students based on an attribute (e.g., G2 >= 10).
